**Effective September 18, 2026**

Thanksgiving Journal is designed to keep your journal private.

## Your journal data

Your journal data (blessing list, prayer-list entries, and personalized Everyday Blessing) are stored locally within the app on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer or shared with third parties.

This information may be included in device backups you choose to make through Apple, depending on your iCloud or device-backup settings.

## Bible verses and internet access

The app may request Bible verse text from [bible-api.com](https://bible-api.com). These requests contain a Bible reference, such as “Psalm 107:1,” and do not contain your blessings, prayer-list entries, or personalized Everyday Blessing.

Like other internet services, the Bible service may receive technical information associated with a request, such as an IP address and request time. Its handling of that information is governed by its own practices. If the service or your internet connection is unavailable, the app displays a verse bundled with the app.

## Accounts, analytics, and advertising

Thanksgiving Journal does not require an account. The developer does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies in the app.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your blessings, prayer-list entries, and personalized preferences from Settings by selecting **Delete All Journal Data**.

You can also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device. Information contained in an Apple backup is controlled through your Apple backup settings.

## Children’s privacy

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users through Thanksgiving Journal.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
